[#idna-errors]
== Errors

Every operation of idna that can fail returns its error as a value: an
`idna::result<T>`, Boost.System's `result`, or, for `to_unicode`, which
always converts what it can, a struct whose `error` is a
`boost::system::error_code`. idna throws nothing of its own. Its errors are
the values of `idna::error`, of the category `webcpp.idna`, one per family
of rules rather than one per status code of UTS{nbsp}46: IdnaTestV2, Unicode's
own conformance file, says an implementation "need only record that there
is an error". The first error found is the one reported, but for
`output_too_small`: `to_ascii` reports it last, after every label's error,
`bidi` and `domain_too_long`, and the span form of `to_unicode` lets it
replace an earlier error (<<idna-converting>>). The labels are processed in
order, and the checks of a label in the order UTS{nbsp}46 section 4.1 lists
them, IDNA2008's last.

[cols="1,3,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Value | Enumerator | Reports | Message

| 1 | `invalid_utf8` | input that is not well-formed UTF-8 (RFC{nbsp}3629) | invalid UTF-8
| 2 | `disallowed` | UTS{nbsp}46 V7: a status other than valid, or deviation when nontransitional; V5: a U+002E inside one label | disallowed code point
| 3 | `not_idna2008` | a code point UTS{nbsp}46 marks NV8 or XV8, DISALLOWED in IDNA2008 | code point disallowed by IDNA2008
| 4 | `not_ldh` | UTS{nbsp}46 U1: ASCII other than `a` to `z`, `0` to `9` and the hyphen | ASCII code point other than a letter, a digit or a hyphen
| 5 | `not_nfc` | UTS{nbsp}46 V1, RFC{nbsp}5891 section 5.4 | label not in Normalization Form C
| 6 | `hyphen_in_third_and_fourth` | UTS{nbsp}46 V2 | hyphens in the third and fourth positions
| 7 | `hyphen_at_start_or_end` | UTS{nbsp}46 V3 | hyphen at the start or end of a label
| 8 | `ace_prefix` | UTS{nbsp}46 V4: `xn--` while `check_hyphens` is off | label starts with xn--
| 9 | `leading_combining_mark` | UTS{nbsp}46 V6, RFC{nbsp}5891 section 5.4 | label starts with a combining mark
| 10 | `contextj` | IdnaTestV2 C1, C2: RFC{nbsp}5892 A.1, A.2 | joiner outside its allowed context
| 11 | `contexto` | RFC{nbsp}5892 A.3 to A.9 | code point outside its allowed context
| 12 | `bidi` | IdnaTestV2 B1 to B6: RFC{nbsp}5893 section 2 | Bidi rule violated
| 13 | `invalid_punycode` | RFC{nbsp}3492 decoding; UTS{nbsp}46 P4; an A-label not in its canonical form | invalid Punycode
| 14 | `punycode_overflow` | RFC{nbsp}3492 section 6.4; UTS{nbsp}46 A3; a label's Punycode past the conversions' bound of 256 code points (<<idna-limits-punycode>>) | Punycode overflow
| 15 | `empty_label` | UTS{nbsp}46 A4_2 and X4_2 | empty label
| 16 | `label_too_long` | UTS{nbsp}46 A4_2: more than 63 octets | label longer than 63 octets
| 17 | `domain_too_long` | UTS{nbsp}46 A4_1: more than 253 octets | domain name longer than 253 octets
| 18 | `output_too_small` | the caller's span cannot hold the result | output too small
| 19 | `storage_exhausted` | a label beyond the inline working storage, with no memory resource | label beyond the working storage
|===

The values are fixed: none changes, and none is reused for another meaning.
An `idna::error` converts to an `error_code` and compares with one,
`result.error() == idna::error::contexto`.
