[#idna-guarantees]
== Guarantees

Each guarantee keeps its number and its anchor; code, tests and comments cite
them.

. [[idna-invariant-1,guarantee 1]]No span form of a conversion and no
  validator allocates, whatever its input; a pmr form allocates from its
  memory resource alone.
. [[idna-invariant-2,guarantee 2]]No conversion, validator or Punycode
  function throws but the allocation of the memory resource a pmr form was
  given. The category's `message(int)` returns a `std::string`, as
  Boost.System's `error_category` declares it, so it may throw what the
  global `operator new` throws. The member `message(char*, std::size_t)` of
  `error_code`, which calls the category's `message(int, char*,
  std::size_t)`, copies the message into a caller's buffer and throws
  nothing.
. [[idna-invariant-3,guarantee 3]]The same input and options give the same
  output and the same error on every target.
. [[idna-invariant-4,guarantee 4]]With `verify_dns_length`, `to_ascii`
  returns at most `idna::max_domain_length` chars, and reports a name whose
  ASCII form is longer as `domain_too_long`, unless an earlier error comes
  first in the order of the errors (<<idna-converting-to-ascii>>), and never
  as `output_too_small` in a buffer of that size. After an error, the buffer
  of the span form of `to_ascii`, as of a Punycode span form, holds nothing
  to read; the span form of `to_unicode` goes on after an error, as
  UTS{nbsp}46 asks, and the `size` chars of its `unicode_result` are the name
  as the processing left it.
. [[idna-invariant-5,guarantee 5]]The tables are exactly what
  `tools/generate_tables.py` writes from the pinned Unicode {unicode-version}
  files.
