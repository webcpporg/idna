[#idna-validity]
== Validity

`idna::check_label` and `idna::check_domain` say whether a label, or a
domain name, is valid as it is given, by IDNA2008's rules and UTS{nbsp}46's
validity criteria, without UTS{nbsp}46's mapping: nothing is lowercased,
normalized or replaced, so a label with a capital letter, or one not in NFC,
is refused. The one exception is an A-label, whose case does not matter
(below). They are the checks a program needs when a name must already be in
its final form, as a hostname's A-labels must. `is_valid_label` and
`is_valid_domain` say the same as a `bool`.

[source]
----
include::{examples}/idna_validity.cpp[tag=labels,indent=0]
----

An A-label's `+xn--+` and Punycode are read in either case, as RFC{nbsp}5890
compares them. The label must be ASCII, and its Punycode must decode to a
label that holds a code point beyond ASCII (UTS{nbsp}46's P4). Its U-label is
then checked by the rules below, Normalization Form C included, and encoded
again: an A-label that is not the encoding of its U-label, its letters
compared in lowercase, is refused, as RFC{nbsp}5891 section 5.4 asks. Each
failure of the A-label itself, a code point beyond ASCII, Punycode that does
not decode, P4 or the round trip, is `invalid_punycode`, or
`punycode_overflow` for a number past 32 bits. Under
`ignore_invalid_punycode`, a label whose Punycode does not decode is kept as
it is, and only its length is checked.

`check_label` refuses a U+002E inside its label as `disallowed`, and applies
the Bidi rule to its label as to a name of one label. `check_domain` splits
at U+002E alone: the other full stops, U+3002, U+FF0E and U+FF61, separate
labels only after UTS{nbsp}46's mapping, so as given they are code points UTS{nbsp}46
maps, and `disallowed`. It applies the Bidi rule across the labels, and adds
the length of the whole name:

[#idna-validity-rules]
=== The rules

A label is checked in this order, and the first rule it breaks is its error;
`to_ascii` and `to_unicode` apply the same rules to each label after the
mapping, with the NFC check only for a decoded A-label:

. *Normalization Form C* (UTS{nbsp}46 V1, RFC{nbsp}5891 section 5.4): `not_nfc`.
. *Hyphens*, under `check_hyphens`: none in both the third and the fourth
  position (V2), `hyphen_in_third_and_fourth`, which keeps such labels free
  for a future prefix, `+xn--+` among them; none at either end (V3),
  `hyphen_at_start_or_end`. Without `check_hyphens`, a label that starts with
  `+xn--+` (V4), `ace_prefix`.
. *No leading combining mark* (V6, RFC{nbsp}5891 section 5.4):
  `leading_combining_mark`.
. *UTS{nbsp}46's status* (V7): every code point valid, or a deviation in
  nontransitional processing; `disallowed`.
. *STD3 ASCII*, under `use_std3_ascii_rules` (U1): ASCII only `a` to `z`,
  `0` to `9` and the hyphen; `not_ldh`.
. *CONTEXTJ*, under `check_joiners` (RFC{nbsp}5892 A.1, A.2): U+200C after a
  virama, or between a code point of Joining_Type L or D and one of R or D,
  with only T between; U+200D after a virama; `contextj`.
. *IDNA2008*, under `idna2008`: no code point UTS{nbsp}46 marks NV8 or XV8,
  `not_idna2008`; then the CONTEXTO rules, `contexto`: U+00B7 MIDDLE DOT
  between two `l` (A.3), U+0375 GREEK KERAIA before a Greek code point
  (A.4), U+05F3 and U+05F4, GERESH and GERSHAYIM, after a Hebrew one (A.5,
  A.6), U+30FB KATAKANA MIDDLE DOT in a label that holds Hiragana, Katakana
  or Han (A.7), and Arabic-Indic digits never beside extended ones (A.8,
  A.9).

Across the labels of a name, under `check_bidi`: when one label holds an R,
AL or AN code point, the name is a Bidi domain name, and then every label
must satisfy RFC{nbsp}5893 section 2, `bidi`. `0a.א` fails: its first label
starts with a digit, which rule 1 refuses in a Bidi domain name. Under
`verify_dns_length`, the lengths of the ASCII form: each label's with the
label, an empty one as `empty_label` and, after its rules, one of more than
63 octets as `label_too_long`; the whole name's last, after the Bidi rule,
`domain_too_long`. A label that outgrows the working storage of 256 code
points, which no label within the DNS lengths does, is refused as soon as it
does, before its rules: `label_too_long` under `verify_dns_length`,
`storage_exhausted` without it (<<idna-errors>>).

idna derives IDNA2008's property of a code point from UTS{nbsp}46's table: PVALID,
CONTEXTJ or CONTEXTO exactly when its status is valid or deviation and it
bears no NV8 or XV8 mark. The generator checks this against IANA's
IDNA2008 tables each time it runs (<<idna-unicode>>).
